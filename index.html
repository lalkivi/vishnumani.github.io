About Me

“Never let go of the child in you who is curious, motivated, and determined to succeed no matter how many times you fail.”

As a kid, my fascination with biological systems started at home watching my mother prepare anatomical notes and medical illustrations for her clinical lectures. I was captivated by how intricately coordinated biological systems are to produce natural human movement.

My passion for mechanical systems sparked with my first bicycle—specifically its back-pedal coaster brake. It was an eye-opener: a designer can dictate system behavior through clever mechanical architecture.

That curiosity led me abroad to forge my career as a robotics engineer. Since then, I have designed and engineered walking robotic systems, including rehabilitation exoskeletons for spinal cord injury mobility, augmentative exoskeletons for aging populations, and next-generation humanoid robots at IHMC.
