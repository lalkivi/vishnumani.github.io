Background & Philosophy

“Never let go of the child in you who is curious, motivated, and determined to succeed no matter how many times you fail.”

— Vishnu Aishwaryan Subra Mani

Growing up watching my mother prepare anatomical illustrations for medical school lectures sparked my lifelong fascination with how biological systems seamlessly coordinate human movement. Later, discovering the mechanics of a back-pedal bicycle brake revealed that mechanical design can dictate physical behavior entirely.

Driven to unite these domains, I moved abroad to build my identity as a robotics engineer. Today, my work focuses on bridging biological elegance with robotic hardware—designing walking systems, augmentative exoskeletons, and agile humanoids that replicate natural biomechanical efficiency.
